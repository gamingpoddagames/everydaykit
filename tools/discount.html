How discount calculator works

A discount calculator tells you how much you save and what you pay after applying a percentage discount. It is useful for shopping, budgeting and comparing offers.

How is the sale price calculated?

The savings are original price multiplied by the discount rate, then savings are subtracted from the original price.

Can I enter more than 100%?

A normal discount should be between 0% and 100%, so the calculator rejects values outside that range.
