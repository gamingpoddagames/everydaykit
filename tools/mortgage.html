How mortgage calculator works

A mortgage calculator estimates the principal-and-interest payment on a fixed-rate mortgage. It does not include property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA fees or lender-specific costs.

Why can my actual payment be higher?

Taxes, insurance, mortgage insurance and other costs may be added to principal and interest.
