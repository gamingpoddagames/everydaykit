How compound interest calculator works

Compound interest lets interest earn additional interest over time. This calculator uses the standard compound-growth formula and shows the future value and interest earned.

Does this include regular deposits?

No. This version models a starting balance without recurring contributions.

Why does compounding frequency matter?

More frequent compounding can slightly increase growth because interest is added to the balance more often.
