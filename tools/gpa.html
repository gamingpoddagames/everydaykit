How gpa calculator works

A GPA calculator estimates a weighted grade-point average. Enter grade points for each course and the corresponding credit values in matching order, separated by commas.

Is this a universal GPA scale?

No. The calculator accepts grade-point numbers you provide. Schools may use different scales and rules for weighting.
