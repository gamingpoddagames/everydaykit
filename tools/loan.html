How loan payment calculator works

A loan payment calculator estimates the regular payment for an amortizing loan. Enter the principal, annual interest rate and term. The result shows the monthly payment, total amount paid and estimated interest.

Does this include fees?

No. It estimates principal and interest only. Lender fees, taxes and insurance can change the real payment.

What if the interest rate is zero?

The calculator divides the loan amount evenly across the number of monthly payments.
