How calorie calculator works

This calculator estimates basal metabolic rate with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and applies an activity multiplier. It is a general estimate, not medical advice.

Are the results exact?

No. Energy needs vary between people. Treat the estimate as a starting point rather than a precise prescription.
