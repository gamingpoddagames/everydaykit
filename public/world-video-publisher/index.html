World Video Publisher

World Video Publisher is an owner-operated tool for preparing original Sinhala-language news video summaries with accurate text, authorized visuals, and narration. The owner reviews videos before publication when a platform requires creator consent.

How it works

Articles are checked for relevance and repetition. The workflow creates a branded video using licensed footage when available, relevant approved archive photos, or original factual news graphics. News videos can be sent to accounts that have explicitly authorized the relevant platform's official API. Uploading a TikTok draft does not automatically make the video publicly visible.

Public TikTok direct posting is only enabled with suitable account authorization, app approval, and creator review. These integrations may be unavailable while the app is in review.

Account control

Only an authorized account owner can connect a social account. The owner can revoke TikTok permissions in TikTok settings. We do not provide a service to publish to accounts without authorization.
