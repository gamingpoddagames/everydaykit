Privacy Policy

This policy describes privacy practices for the World Video Publisher website and its owner-authorized TikTok news-video integration. Some features are in development or awaiting platform approval.

1. Information that may be processed

When a creator connects TikTok, the integration may receive a TikTok authorization code, access and refresh tokens, account identifier (open_id), basic profile information permitted by the authorized scopes, and publication identifiers/status. We may process article text, captions, media files, and technical logs needed to create posts. Website hosting may process routine request metadata including IP addresses to serve and protect the site.

2. How the information is used

We use authorized information solely to verify the selected publishing account, create or upload requested videos, check draft or publication status, prevent duplicate posts, maintain security and troubleshoot errors. TikTok API data is not used to sell profiles or send unrelated messages.

3. Storage and access

Publishing tokens, when configured, are intended for secured server-side secrets or encrypted authorization storage, not public web pages or GitHub source code. A restricted publishing history may retain news story IDs, TikTok publish IDs and statuses needed to avoid repeat uploads. Access is limited to the account operator and infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service. Retention may vary with platform requirements and operational needs.

4. Sharing and third parties

The integration transfers the video, post information and required authorizations to TikTok only when the account owner authorizes the relevant action. Website hosting and authorized infrastructure providers process technical information as service providers. TikTok's own Privacy Policy governs its platform processing.

5. Account choices and deletion

You can revoke TikTok app access within TikTok security settings. For requests to review or delete information held by this operator, use the contact page, providing enough information to identify the account without sending passwords or tokens. TikTok posts or drafts may require separate deletion in TikTok.

6. Children

This tool is intended for creators permitted to operate a TikTok account and developer integration under TikTok's terms. It is not designed to solicit personal information from children.

7. Security and updates

We use access-controlled credentials where supported. No online system can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated if service features, legal requirements, or information practices change; the effective date will be revised.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, use the EverydayKit contact page.
