Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the current FreeSignal Video Publisher website and separate owner-operated publishing workflow handle information.

1. Public caption tool

The public caption builder operates in your browser. Text entered into the caption editor is not deliberately transmitted to our application server or stored in a FreeSignal database by this tool. Like other hosted sites, the web hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver pages and maintain security.

2. Social platform authorization

Currently the public website does not request TikTok sign-in. If a TikTok login and posting integration is later enabled, the account owner will be shown TikTok's authorization screen. The separate publishing workflow is designed to use authorized access tokens and account information needed to verify the intended creator and submit videos. Access tokens are intended to be stored in GitHub Actions Secrets, not in public code or captions.

3. Information used by the publishing workflow

When the authorized owner enables publishing, the workflow may process creator username, creator posting settings, generated video files, captions, post identifiers, publication status, and timestamps. These are used for account verification, posting, duplicate prevention, and troubleshooting.

4. Sharing and security

Information needed for authorized posting is sent to the relevant platform API, including TikTok or Meta as applicable. Repository-based automation uses a third-party hosting and automation provider, GitHub. We do not sell visitor data or use the public caption tool to collect passwords or payment-card information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Retention and user choices

Local browser caption text remains in your current page only unless you independently copy or save it. Publishing receipts may be kept by the owner-controlled workflow to prevent duplicate posts and manage publishing records. The owner can revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok's settings; revocation prevents future authorized posting after token invalidation. Deleting copies of previously published videos may require action on the platform.

6. Children and updates

This website is not designed to gather information from children. Policy updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact

For public information, visit FreeSignal Trading Alerts. Please do not share personal information, tokens or private data requests in a public channel. A private contact process for privacy requests must be established before offering broad third-party account integrations.
